Pin pi-btw 0.63.5, pi-antigravity 0.12.0, and pi-lens 4.4.10. BTW loads its fullscreen UI on first use and requires pi-tui-kit ^0.69.1. Antigravity keeps a refreshed catalog from restoring a removed model and rejects image symlink destinations. pi-lens lists Groovy classes and methods, and read widening can be turned off without disabling the read-before-edit guard.
